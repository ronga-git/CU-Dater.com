Age Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

18+ Requirement

CU-Dater is intended exclusively for users who are 18 years of age or older. By downloading or using the App, you confirm that you meet this age requirement.

CU-Dater is a personal dating-life organizer. It helps adults keep track of their connections, set reminders, and manage their dating activity. It is not suitable for minors.

Parental Guidance

We strongly encourage parents and guardians to monitor the apps installed on devices used by minors. If you believe a person under 18 is using CU-Dater, please contact us immediately.

Enforcement

The App does not collect personal information that would allow us to verify a user's age independently. Users are responsible for complying with this age requirement. We reserve the right to terminate access for any user found to be under 18.
