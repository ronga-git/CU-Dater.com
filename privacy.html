Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

CU-Dater ("the App") is a free Android application developed by SPCoolApps. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the App and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

CU-Dater does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information. SPCoolApps does not collect, receive, or store any of your personal data. We have no servers that receive your entries.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Everything you enter in the App (such as names, ages, origin app, priorities, notes, dates, and reminders) and your preferences are stored locally on your device only, using Android's standard storage. This data:

  • Is never transmitted to SPCoolApps or shared with any third party by us
  • Is not used for advertising
  • Is deleted when you uninstall the App, unless you have made a backup

Your entries may include information about other people. Please record only what is appropriate for your personal use. See our Terms of Service.

3. Backup and Sharing

The App lets you export a backup file of your data, and share content with other apps using Android's share menu. These actions happen only when you choose them. The file or content goes to the location or app you select (for example, your device storage, a cloud drive, or a messaging app), and is then handled under that service's own privacy policy. Backup files are not encrypted, so please store them securely.

4. Advertising (Meta Audience Network)

The App displays ads served by Meta Audience Network (operated by Meta Platforms, Inc.) to keep it free. To serve and measure ads, Meta may collect and use certain device information, including:

  • Android Advertising ID
  • Device type, model, and OS version
  • IP address and approximate location (country/region level)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

Meta does not receive the content you enter in the App. This data is collected and processed by Meta, not by SPCoolApps, in accordance with the Meta Privacy Policy.

You can limit personalised advertising on your device under Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads) by deleting or resetting your Advertising ID.

5. In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional one-time purchase to remove ads. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. SPCoolApps never receives or stores your payment card or billing details. The App only receives confirmation from Google Play that a purchase was made, so it can turn off ads and restore the purchase on your devices. Payment information is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Third-Party Services

The App uses only the following third-party services:

  • Meta Audience Network, for advertising (see section 4)
  • Google Play Billing, for the optional purchase (see section 5)

No other analytics, tracking, crash reporting, or social media SDKs are used.

7. Children's Privacy

CU-Dater is intended only for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a minor has used the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate action. See our Age Policy and Child Safety Standards.

8. Data Security

Since SPCoolApps does not collect or transmit your personal data, there is no risk of a data breach involving your entries through our systems. Your data remains on your device under your control. Protecting your device with a screen lock helps keep it private.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Because SPCoolApps does not hold personal data about you, you can delete all App data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage. Requests about advertising data should be directed to Meta through the link above. You are welcome to contact us with any privacy question.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
