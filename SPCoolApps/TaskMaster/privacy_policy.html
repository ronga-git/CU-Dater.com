Privacy Policy — TaskMaster-GO

Effective Date: November 5, 2026
Developer: SPCoolApps
App Name: TaskMaster-GO (Android and iOS)
Contact: info@cu-dater.com

1. Overview

TaskMaster-GO is a personal productivity application designed to help users organize, prioritize, and track their tasks. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles user data. We are committed to transparency and full compliance with Apple App Store and Google Play Store policies.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

SPCoolApps does not collect, store, or receive any of the following through TaskMaster-GO:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Precise location data
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Contacts or calendar data
  • Financial or payment information
  • Health or fitness data
  • Browsing history or search history
  • User credentials or passwords

No account or registration is required to use this application.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All task data you create within the app (task names, categories, statuses, colors, counts, and prices) is stored exclusively on your device. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is never transmitted to any server
  • Is never shared with SPCoolApps or any third party
  • Is deleted when you uninstall the application

4. Advertising (Meta Audience Network)

TaskMaster-GO displays advertisements provided by Meta Audience Network (operated by Meta Platforms, Inc.) to keep the application free for all users. To serve and measure ads, Meta may collect and use certain device information, including:

  • Advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
  • Device type, model, and OS version
  • IP address and general location (country/region level, derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Meta, not by SPCoolApps. For full details:

  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • Meta Audience Network

You can limit ad personalization at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads), then delete or reset your Advertising ID.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The app asks for your permission before any tracking on iOS.

5. In-App Purchases

TaskMaster-GO offers an optional one-time purchase to remove ads. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. SPCoolApps never receives or stores your payment card or billing details. The app only receives confirmation from the store that a purchase was made, so it can turn off ads and restore the purchase on your devices. Your payment information is handled under the Google Privacy Policy or the Apple Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy

TaskMaster-GO is not directed at children under the age of 13, and SPCoolApps does not knowingly collect any data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us and we will take immediate action.

7. Data Security

Since SPCoolApps does not collect or transmit personal user data, there is no risk of a data breach involving your personal information through our systems. All task data remains on your device under your full control.

8. Third-Party Services

TaskMaster-GO integrates the following third-party services only:

  • Meta Audience Network, for advertising (see section 4)
  • Google Play Billing (Android) and Apple In-App Purchase (iOS), for the optional purchase (see section 5)

No other analytics, tracking, crash reporting, or social media SDKs are used.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Because SPCoolApps does not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data should be directed to Meta through the links above. You are welcome to contact us with any privacy question.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Effective Date" at the top of this document. Continued use of the application after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
